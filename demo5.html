About FoodSpice

Our Story, Spiced Up.

Located in the heart of Lalpur, FoodSpice is a contemporary dining space that celebrates Ranchi’s vibrant culture on every plate. We handpick our spices from local markets and blend them with modern cooking techniques to create an experience that feels both familiar and exciting.

From the smoky richness of Handi Mutton to delicate Italian pastas, our chefs craft each dish to balance bold flavor with refined presentation – perfect for family dinners, date nights, or weekend catch-ups.
